Consistency of signals with each hypothesis

Analysis of competing hypotheses: each signal against each hypothesis. C means consistent (cited for the hypothesis), I means inconsistent (cited against it), and a dash means not applicable. The hypothesis with the fewest I marks survives best; C marks count for less, because one signal can fit several hypotheses.

Claim labels: Confirmed means two or more independent originators, or the victim or an authority (government, regulator or law enforcement) speaking about its own actions. Reported means one credible originator; an allegation against someone not convicted stays here. Actor-asserted means the attacker said it about itself, which shows only that the claim was made. Assessed is the analyst's judgment, with a likelihood and confidence. Superseded means later reporting replaced it, and it is kept on purpose. Unknown is a gap that matters.
