On 21 October 2026 at 14:05 UTC we detected unusual activity on our FerryGate file transfer server.
Attackers exploited a vulnerability in our FerryGate file transfer server on 14 October.
We isolated the affected server on 22 October and engaged an outside forensic firm.
Some customer names and email addresses were copied from the server.
No payment card data was affected.
